Fat Cat Todo – Privacy Policy

Fat Cat Todo · Last updated: 23 November 2026
Terms of UseTiếng Việttoonred.com

Fat Cat Todo (“the app”) is a to-do and habit app for Android and iOS made by ToonRed Studio (Toon App Studio, Hanoi, Vietnam – toonred.com). This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Your tasks stay on your device

Task titles, notes, dates, reminders, settings and everything else you type are stored only on your device. They are not uploaded to any server and we have no account system. The home-screen widgets read the same local data. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted. You can export or delete it at any time in More → Settings → Data.

2. Anonymous usage statistics (optional)

To understand how the app is used and improve it, the app uses Google Firebase Analytics, only while the setting “Share anonymous usage data” is on. You choose during onboarding and can turn it off any time in Settings.

When enabled, Firebase collects:

  • App interactions: screens opened and events such as “task added”, “task completed”, “theme changed”, “paywall viewed”, “purchase started/completed”. The text of your tasks is never sent.
  • App and device information: app version, OS version, device model, language, approximate country (derived from IP by Google), and an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase.

The app does not collect the Android Advertising ID or Apple’s IDFA, does not track you across other apps or websites, does not show ads, and never sells data. This data is not linked to your identity. It is sent encrypted (HTTPS) to Google and kept according to Firebase’s retention settings (default 14 months). See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

3. Purchases

Fat Cat Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is sold through Google Play Billing on Android and the App Store (Apple In-App Purchase) on iOS. Payment details are handled entirely by Google or Apple; the app only receives confirmation of which Pro product you own.

4. Notifications

Reminders and the daily briefing are scheduled locally on your device. Notification permission is requested only when you turn on a reminder or the daily briefing.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Turn off usage statistics at any time in Settings → Data.
  • Delete all local data in Settings → Data → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • To ask about or delete analytics data associated with your app instance, contact us (below).

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
