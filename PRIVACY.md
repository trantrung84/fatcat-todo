# Fat Cat Todo – Privacy Policy

> The official, up-to-date version is published at https://toonred.com/fatcat-todo/privacy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Fat Cat Todo ("the app") is a to-do and habit app made by **ToonRed Studio** (Toon App Studio, Hanoi, Vietnam – https://toonred.com). This policy explains what data the app handles.

## 1. Your tasks stay on your device
Task titles, notes, dates, reminders, settings and everything else you type are stored **only on your device** (in the app's local storage). They are not uploaded to any server. The home-screen widget reads the same local data. If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted. You can export or delete it at any time in **More → Settings → Data**.

## 2. Anonymous usage statistics (optional)
To understand how the app is used and improve it, the app uses **Google Firebase Analytics**, only while the setting **"Share anonymous usage data"** is on. You can choose during onboarding and turn it off any time in Settings.

When enabled, Firebase collects:
- **App interactions**: screens opened and events such as "task added", "task completed", "theme changed", "paywall viewed", "purchase started/completed". **The text of your tasks is never sent.**
- **App and device information**: app version, OS version, device model, language, approximate country (derived from IP by Google), and an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase.

The app **does not** collect the Android Advertising ID, does not show ads, and does not use data for advertising or sell it. Data is sent encrypted (HTTPS) to Google and is kept according to Firebase's retention settings (default 14 months). See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 3. Purchases
Fat Cat Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is sold through **Google Play Billing**. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; the app only receives confirmation of which Pro product you own. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

## 4. Notifications
Reminders and the daily briefing are scheduled locally on your device. Notification permission is requested only when you turn on a reminder or the daily briefing.

## 5. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## 6. Your choices and rights
- Turn off usage statistics at any time in Settings → Data.
- Delete all local data in Settings → Data → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app.
- To ask about or delete analytics data associated with your app instance, contact us (below).

## 7. Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
